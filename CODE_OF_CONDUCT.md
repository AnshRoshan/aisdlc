# Code of Conduct

Applies to issues, pull requests, advisories, the site, and any space where this project is discussed.

**Argue with the artifact, not the person.** The whole point of this kit is that a claim is checkable:
paste the command, the exit code, the evidence record. "It should work" and "you are wrong" are equally
unhelpful here. If you disagree with a design decision, quote the decision — `context/decisions.md` and
`docs/` are in the repo for exactly that reason.

**Do not push on someone else's gate.** Nobody is obliged to sign your approval, merge your PR, or answer
on a schedule. A maintainer who says "not now" is not blocking you.

**Never fake green.** Do not present an unrun test, an estimated number, or a screenshot of a hypothetical
result as evidence — in a contribution or in a discussion. This project's own rules require that a
measurement names the run it came from or is marked NOT RUN.

**Keep secrets out of public threads.** Issues, PR comments and advisories are not a place to paste tokens,
`.env` contents, or a reproducer that embeds a credential. Use placeholders the scanner already recognises,
and redact before posting.

**Harassment, slurs, and gatekeeping of any kind are not welcome**, whoever they come from, including from
people who are right about the technical point.

**Reporting.** Write to the maintainer privately — through a
[security advisory](https://github.com/AnshRoshan/arsdlc/security/advisories/new) or any channel that
reaches a human. This is a one-person project: expect an acknowledgement, not a committee, and expect the
reasoning to be stated plainly. Consequences scale from a request to edit a comment, to a block from the
repo's discussion surfaces.
